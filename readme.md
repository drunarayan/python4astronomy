## Premise 
This course is taught upon request from the friends and staff of Boyce Astro Foundation Staff & Friends by Chandru Narayan. As such this course will primarily focus on the Python & Plotting using JupyterLab, Markdown, LaTeX, Data Frames, Gaia Queries, latest Computing concepts, and some maths taught in the original course. Although, we will deemphasise defining common Astromical terms, the complete details and original course content in the form of Jupyter Notebooks is [available here](readme_020725_saved) for you explore and download as needed. ## Infrastructure
All content of this course is created on a Google Virtual Machine (VM) and delivered on a html5 compatible Browser such as Chrome or Firefox. Absolutely no local computer downloads will be needed or encouraged including the Jupyter Notebooks themselves. Your notebooks will be available to edit & run from your individual logins on JupyterHub and can be accessed when the Google VM is scheduled to be up and available in your respective time zones. However, you may prefer to save the Jupyter Notebooks on your laptop for future reference once the course is complete.  This course designed to be taken on a laptop/desktop and possibly on an iPad. However, it cannot be properly taken on a smart phone.
